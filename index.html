Questions, answered plainly

The things people ask before they email.

What does a website cost?

The packages above show honest starting prices — a Website from 390,000 kr, a Direct Booking Site from 590,000 kr, a custom app from 320,000 kr, AI Automation from 190,000 kr setup plus 39,000 kr/month. Your exact quote is fixed in writing in the discovery plan and won’t change unless the scope does. If it isn’t in your budget, you keep the plan and owe nothing.

Why not just use a cheap AI chatbot subscription?

Generic chatbot SaaS is cheap because it’s generic — trained on nothing specific to you, connected to nothing you already use, and billed forever. What we build is trained on your business, connects to the systems you already run, and for apps, you can own the result outright instead of renting it every month. If a $99/month tool genuinely covers what you need, we’ll say so on the discovery call — we’d rather lose the sale than sell you something you don’t need.

Do I need to understand AI to use the automation?

No. You’ll see the results — answered questions, drafted replies, sent reminders — in tools you already use. The training session covers everything in plain language, and the monthly note tells you what ran and what changed.

Can you work with our existing booking system?

Usually, yes. Most projects connect to what you already have — booking engines, channel managers, point-of-sale — rather than replacing it. If your current system genuinely can’t support what you need, you’ll hear that in the discovery plan, with options.

What happens if we stop working together?

Nothing breaks. You own the code, the domain, the content, and the accounts from day one, and everything is documented so any developer can pick it up. The retainer is convenience, not a hostage situation.

We’re not in Iceland. Can we still work with you?

Yes — the studio is based in Húsavík but works with businesses across Iceland and Europe, in English or Spanish. Everything from discovery to launch works over video calls and shared links.

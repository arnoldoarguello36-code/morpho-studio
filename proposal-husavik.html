Where things stand today

We looked at hotelhusavik.is before writing a single line of this.

Nothing below is a guess — it's what the current site actually does, room by room.

01

Built on a shared template

The site runs on SiteMinder's "Canvas" builder — the same layout, carousel, and widget hundreds of other small hotels use. It's tidy, but there's nothing in it that says this is Húsavík, specifically.

02

Six room types, one seasonal deal

Deluxe Sea View, Superior Mountain View, Standard, Economy, Triple, and Family — plus the Winter Deal with GeoSea, from 19,900 ISK/person/night. All static HTML: changing a price or a promo means editing the page itself.

03

Booking handed off to a widget

Arrival, departure, and guest count are collected on-site, then the guest is passed to a third-party booking engine before ever seeing a room or a price — the exact friction point where OTAs win the booking instead.

04

No visible link to godo

There's nothing on the public site that shows rates or availability coming from godo in real time — which usually means someone on staff is updating prices in two or three places by hand, and one of them eventually falls out of sync.

What we'd build instead

One site, three jobs, wired together.

This is Morpho's Direct Booking Site package, scoped specifically to Hótel Húsavík's six room types and its relationship with godo.

Pillar 01

A booking engine that talks to godo

Rates and availability are read from godo, not typed in twice — and every booking made on the site is pushed back to godo the moment it's confirmed. A room sold on the website is a room godo already knows is gone, so it can't be sold again through an OTA five minutes later.

Pillar 02

One screen for prices and promotions

Change a nightly rate, open or close a room type, launch a seasonal offer like the Winter Deal — from a plain dashboard, published instantly. No developer, no template edit, no waiting on an agency abroad.

Pillar 03

Payment inside the booking flow

Card capture — deposit or full stay — at the moment of booking, in ISK with EUR/USD support for international guests. A confirmed booking becomes a paid one, without ever leaving hotelhusavik.is.

Admin dashboard — illustrative numbers, not a projection. The point is the shape: a glance at occupancy and channel mix, and a rate you can edit in place.

Palette — arctic navy as the base, with a glacier-blue and aurora-green accent pair. Distinct from the generic template blue, and pulled from the actual place: Skjálfandi Bay, GeoSea, the northern lights.

Type — a geometric sans for structure (nav, prices, forms) paired with an editorial serif, set in italic, for room names and trip stories — so a "Deluxe Double with Sea View" reads like a place, not a database row.

Motion — a tide-style progress indicator across the three booking steps instead of a generic form wizard — a small, specific touch that a stock template can't replicate.

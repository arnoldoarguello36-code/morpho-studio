About us

Four hundred years by this bay, three generations behind the desk.

We're the Örlygsson family — hoteliers now, but fishermen and farmers before that. Our great-grandparents grew up on opposite shores of Skjálfandi Bay, on farms with no road between them, long before anyone came here to watch whales. In 1945 the family crossed the bay and settled in Húsavík itself, and we've stayed close to the town ever since — on the town council, in parliament, and eventually behind the front desk.

We opened as a guesthouse in 2009, became a hotel two years later, and built the town's Eurovision Exhibition and Exploration Museum along the way — because if you're going to run a small hotel in the Arctic, you might as well fill it with the things you actually love. Guests get 10% off food and drinks at Jaja Ding Dong, the bar we run next door.
